WHAT THE MARKET APPEARS TO PROVE

Four monetizable jobs sit around the same data foundation.

01 / TARGET

Make external audiences more valuable

Purchase, identity and behavioural data calibrate who the platform should find. The buyer pays when this lowers CAC or improves predicted LTV.

Examples: Hightouch, Attain, Proxima
02 / OPTIMIZE

Change the value passed into the bidder

First-party outcomes and predicted value steer acquisition, budget and retail-media decisions without rebuilding the platform auction.

Examples: Voyantis, Moloco, CommerceIQ
03 / VERIFY

Replace attribution with causal confidence

Incrementality, MMM and cross-channel evidence still command enterprise spend, but the workflow acts after most product and audience choices are made.

Examples: AppsFlyer, Haus, Measured
04 / CONVERT

Own the experience after the click

Personalized storefronts and post-purchase offers monetize directly through higher conversion, subscription fees or a share of commerce value.

Examples: Rokt, FERMÀT, Replo

VALUATION DISCIPLINE

Value the priced event—not the funding headline.

Every card preserves the source's original metric and links directly to the underlying evidence.

01 / Start with a priced event

Use an explicitly disclosed post-money valuation, secondary transaction, acquisition price or public market value. A round size alone is not a valuation.

02 / Build only a rough multiple

Divide valuation by same-period ARR or revenue only when both are available. Keep ARR, ad-spend revenue, run-rate and exit proceeds visibly separate.

03 / Follow the payer

Named customers prove adoption, not concentration. Unless a filing identifies the largest account, the cards say that concentration is undisclosed.
